Listening settings & tips

Play near the microphone in a quiet room. Use headphones if you play a backing track. If quiet notes disappear, lower the noise gate. If the input clips, move farther away.

The display keeps the last clear note visible for one second through brief dropouts, labeled Last heard. Those retained readings do not count toward a match. Live detections remain on the pitch trail for six seconds.

Chords are estimates: all listed pitches must be detected to match. Strong overtones, octave chords, breath noise and room echoes can confuse the listener. It hears pitch, not your hole or breath technique. Use Next if a chord won’t lock.

Follow along counts you in, then moves through the phrase even when a note doesn’t match. Watch the moving line, upcoming notes and Hold/Rest cues. Step by step waits for a match and a brief breath break. Next skips a step without stopping practice. Speed changes timing, not pitch.
