## How to practice

1. Locate holes 1–10, from the low-pitched end to the high-pitched end.
2. Play one hole at a time. `4` means blow hole 4; `-4` means draw hole 4.
3. Each apostrophe means one semitone of bend. An `o` marks an advanced over-note; the instruction column gives its breath direction.
4. Start at **75% speed**. Use the hold times below and repeat each phrase until you can connect the notes smoothly.
5. Increase your speed gradually, then join neighboring phrases.
